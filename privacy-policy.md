# Privacy Policy — Parking Autopilot

_Last updated: 15 August 2026_

## English

**Parking Autopilot collects no personal data.** There is no server behind this app. Nothing you do in it is sent to us, because there is no "us" to send it to.

### What stays on your phone

| Data | Why | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | To detect that you parked, and that you returned to your car | On the device. Never transmitted |
| Motion & fitness | To tell driving apart from walking, so you aren't asked to park while on foot | On the device. Never transmitted |
| Settings, vehicle list, activity log | To run the app | On the device (app storage) |
| Your parking-service sign-in token | To stay signed in | iOS Keychain, on the device |

Parking places are matched to your location entirely on your phone, against a catalogue of Tbilisi's parking places included in the app. No location lookup leaves the device.

### What is sent, and to whom

The app is a client for Tbilisi's municipal parking service (`parking.tbilisi.gov.ge`, `api.municipal.gov.ge`), operated by the Tbilisi municipality. When you sign in and use it, these go to that service, on your own account:

- your sign-in, through the service's own web login
- the parking place code and the vehicle, when a session starts
- the request to stop a session
- requests to read your balance, vehicles and parking history

That is the same data the official app or website sends for the same actions, and the service's own privacy policy governs it. We receive none of it.

### What the app does not do

- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs
- No data is sold, shared or used for profiling
- No account is created with us — there is no account with us to create

### Permissions you can refuse

Location, notifications and motion are each asked for separately and explained in the app. Refusing any of them leaves the rest working; without location, parking is started and stopped manually. You can change them any time in iOS Settings.

### Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

### Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone, including the sign-in token. Data held by the parking service is theirs — contact them to have it removed.
